# ADR 0005: Business is Membros, not an agency

Business sells seats: a Criador on that Plano invites **Membros** who run Análise, Ideia, and Roteiro. The Criador still pays and owns the Assinatura. This is not an agency desk — Membros belong to one Criador, not to many clients. Canal stays out of the core (ADR 0004); the upgrade is people on the same Limite, which resets on the calendar month.
